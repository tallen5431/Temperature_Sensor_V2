How it works

Three steps, no account, no phone tag.

1

Send a photo

From your phone, right now. The broken part, a ruler next to it if you have one. That's enough to start.

2

I model it and quote

You get a fixed price and a ship date within 24 hours. No charge to look, no obligation.

3

Printed and shipped

Approved parts ship in 3–5 business days. If it doesn't fit, I revise the model and reprint it.

What I make

Obsolete plastic, mostly.

Housings & covers Brackets & mounts Gears Clips & latches Knobs & handles Bezels & trim Spacers & bushings Jigs & fixtures

If the original was injection-moulded plastic and the manufacturer stopped making it, it's probably in scope. Send it and I'll tell you straight whether printing is the right answer — sometimes it isn't, and I'll say so rather than sell you a part that will fail. Load-bearing metal, gas-wetted, or food-contact parts are the usual no.

Price & turnaround

What this usually costs.

Every part is quoted individually before you commit — the numbers below are what most jobs land at, not a rate card you're locked into.

Typical part
$75–250

Most land around $120. The modelling is the real work, so a second identical part is far cheaper than the first — say up front if you need a few.

Turnaround
3–5 days

Business days from your approval to shipped, with the quote back to you within 24 hours. Rush is sometimes possible — ask.

New shops
First part free

Your first part is on me if it's simple geometry — a clip, knob, spacer, bracket or cover. I'll tell you honestly when a part doesn't qualify before doing any work.
