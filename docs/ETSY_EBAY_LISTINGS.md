# Marketplace listing drafts — Etsy & eBay

Ready-to-paste copy for selling custom / replacement 3D-printed parts as **made-to-order**
(no inventory needed — you print each job after it's ordered). Keep prices here in sync with
the `replacement-parts.html` pricing tiers; marketplace prices should run ~10–15 % above the
direct-site prices to cover fees.

> **Etsy fees, precisely** (they are charged on the item **plus** shipping, which is the part most
> calculators miss): $0.20 listing + 6.5 % transaction + 3 % + $0.25 processing — about **12 %** of a
> $39 order shipped at $6. **Offsite Ads** adds **15 %** (dropping to 12 % once your shop passes
> $10k in a 365-day window) but *only* on orders that arrive from an offsite ad click, taking those
> to roughly **27 %**. Below $10k/yr you can opt out of Offsite Ads entirely; at $10k enrolment
> becomes permanent. **eBay** is ≈ 13 % (final value fee + fixed per-order charge).

> **You still need photos.** A listing can't be image-less. Use capability shots: sample
> prints, the printer, close-ups of finished parts, and a before/after of a demo part
> (the wrench + calibration-marker + printed replacement). The same shots seed the site's
> capability-demo section.

## Selling made-to-order without inventory — how the mechanics work

- **Etsy:** list a base made-to-order product, or take a **custom order** — the buyer messages,
  you agree a price, and you create a private listing for them. Etsy requires the item to be
  made/designed by you; printed parts you produce qualify.
- **eBay:** list a fixed-price base with **Best Offer**, or send a buyer a **custom offer** after
  they message. There's a dedicated 3D-printing/custom-manufacturing services area.
- Both let you set an honest **handling/processing time** since nothing ships from stock.

## Where to also drive demand (free, high-intent)

- Reddit: r/functionalprint, r/fixit, r/3Dprinting, plus hobby subs — answer "can anyone print
  this?" posts (follow each sub's self-promo rules).
- Facebook groups for vintage appliances, specific car/motorcycle models, RVs, boats.
- Print-job marketplaces: Xometry / Protolabs Network, Craftcloud, Treatstock — inbound RFQs.
- Everything points back to `datumlaboratories.com/replacement-parts` for the quote form.
