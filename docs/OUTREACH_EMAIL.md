# Cold-outreach email — repair shops

Direct-to-shop email for the replacement-parts service. Keep the numbers in sync with the
`replacement-parts.html` pricing tiers and `docs/ETSY_EBAY_LISTINGS.md` (see the "Keep in sync
by hand" note in `site/README.md`).

**How to use it:** personalize the first line, send in small manual batches (10–20/day, not a
blast), and follow up **once** ~4 days later. Real one-to-one email lands far better than bulk.

---

## Subject line (pick one, A/B test)

- `Discontinued part? I'll remake it`
- `{Shop name} — the part you can't get anymore`
- `Obsolete plastic parts, remade in days`

## Email

```
Hi {first name / there},

I run Datum Labs — I reverse-engineer and 3D print the discontinued plastic parts
that leave a repair dead in the water: knobs, clips, gears, brackets, housings,
bezels. Send a photo of the broken original and I'll reply with a fixed price and
a ship date, usually within one business day.

Simple parts (clips, knobs, spacers) start at $29; most run $75–250. Approved
parts ship in 3–5 business days, and if the first one doesn't fit I revise and
reprint at no charge.

Your first simple part is on me — no cost, no obligation — so you can judge the
quality before you commit.

Got a part that's been holding up a job? Reply with a photo, or start here:
datumlaboratories.com/replacement-parts

— Thomas Allen
Datum Labs · thomas.allen@datumlaboratories.com
```

## Follow-up (send ~4 days later if no reply)

```
Hi {first name}, just circling back — the offer to remake your first simple part
free still stands. Even if nothing's broken today, keep me in your back pocket for
the next discontinued-part headache: a clear photo is all I need to quote it.

— Thomas
```

---

## Notes

- **Personalize line 1** — mention the shop's specialty ("saw you do a lot of vintage
  appliance work") — it roughly doubles reply rates versus a generic opener.
- **CAN-SPAM (US):** a commercial email must include a real physical mailing address and a
  working opt-out. For one-to-one manual sends, add a short line, e.g.:
  `Datum Labs · {your mailing address} · Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.`
- **Targeting:** appliance, small-engine, vacuum, power-tool, RC/hobby, and marine/RV repair
  shops feel the "discontinued plastic part" pain most.
- **Track** replies in a simple sheet (shop, date sent, follow-up date, outcome) so you know
  which subject line and specialty convert.
